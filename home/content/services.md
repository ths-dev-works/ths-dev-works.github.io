I don’t just sell code or infrastructure. I sell **peace of mind**—knowing your technology scales, is secure, and won't cost a fortune.

I operate at the intersection of high-performance **backend development** and **infrastructure operations** (SRE). This duality allows me to design systems that are maintainable from day one.

## What I Do

### 1. Fractional CTO (Startups & SMEs)
Ideal for companies needing senior technical leadership but not yet ready to justify (or find) a full-time CTO.
* **Tech Stack Definition:** Pragmatic choices to avoid over-engineering.
* **Stack Redundancy Analysis:** Identify and eliminate redundant tools or services to reduce complexity and costs.
* **Technical Hiring:** I help filter and interview engineers for your permanent team.
* **Technical Debt Management:** Audit existing code and plan refactoring without stalling the business.
* **Roadmap Alignment:** Translating business goals into actionable engineering tasks.

### 2. Platform Engineering & SRE
I turn chaotic infrastructure into stable platforms. Focus on automation to eliminate human error.
* **Cloud Cost Optimization:** Cost analysis (AWS/GCP) and resource optimization.
* **CI/CD Pipelines:** Robust pipeline configuration (GitHub Actions/GitLab) for fearless deploys.
* **Kubernetes & Containerization:** Cluster migration and management for high availability.
* **Observability:** Implementing metrics, logs, and alerts (Prometheus/Grafana/OpenTelemetry) to catch issues before customers do.

### 3. Go (Golang) Development
When performance and concurrency are critical.
* **Microservices Development:** Fast, typed, and documented APIs.
* **CLI Tools:** Creating internal utilities to accelerate your team's productivity.
* **Legacy Refactoring:** Rewriting performance bottlenecks (Node/Python) in Go.

### 4. AI Solutions Consulting
Pragmatic AI guidance—focus on solving real problems, not chasing hype.
* **AI Use Case Filtering:** Analyze your requirements to determine if AI is actually needed. Many problems are better solved with traditional algorithms, databases, or simple automation.
* **AI Strategy Assessment:** If AI is appropriate, identify the right approach (LLMs, ML models, rule-based systems) and implementation strategy.
* **AI Implementation Planning:** Design and plan AI integrations that are maintainable, cost-effective, and aligned with business goals.
* **AI vs. Traditional Trade-offs:** Help you understand when to use AI and when simpler solutions are more reliable and cost-effective.
